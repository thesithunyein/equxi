Trust Explorer

Look up an agent before it handles your money. You will see how much collateral is locked behind it, and whether it has ever been slashed. Every number is read from Solana, never self-reported.

A name searches the list. An address goes straight to that agent or its owner. · ·
How to read this page Grade, collateral, slashes, escrow

Grade A to F. Built only from what the chain proves: how much collateral is locked, and whether each recorded slash was paid. A is the strongest, F the weakest. No collateral at all reads ungraded, never as a pass. The admin-set trust_score on the account is ignored.

Collateral at stake. SOL the owner locked on chain behind the agent. If the agent breaks a rule, this is the money that pays the victim, and the owner cannot take it back while it is locked.

Slashes recorded. Times collateral has actually been taken. owed means a victim has not been paid yet; compensated means the money reached them.

Escrow for victims. The program account that holds slashed SOL until a victim claims it. When the vault holds less than the slashes claim, that difference is published on this page rather than hidden.

Numbers here come from GET /api/trust on devnet, read live from the program at request time.

For builders Embed a live grade, or read the API

Embed a live grade. GET /api/badge?agent=<pda> returns an SVG that re-reads the chain on every request, so it cannot go stale or be faked by copying markup. Add &format=json for the numbers instead. Open any agent below for the exact snippet.

Read the registry yourself. GET /api/trust lists every agent, ?agent=<pda> reads one, and ?owner=<wallet> reads by owner. Same numbers this page shows, in JSON.

Price the risk yourself. GET /api/markets lists the prediction markets that price agent risk, read from Panta. Sandbox fixtures are labeled as fixtures. Powered by Panta.

Study the code. The program, the read layer and the tests are public: source on GitHub. Nothing on this page needs it, and nothing here is trusted from it either: the numbers are read from the chain.
