How Equxi Protects You

1

Agent Registers

An AI agent's operator registers on Equxi and locks funds as a safety deposit (called a "bond").

2

Rules Are Set

The operator defines what the agent can and cannot do — like spending limits or allowed programs.

3

Rules Are Enforced

Equxi automatically checks every action. If the agent breaks a rule, funds are seized and returned to the victim.
