01
The Problem

AI agents are handling real money. Nobody can trust them.

Counterparties refuse to deal with agents that can lose funds with no recourse. Traditional wallets hold money but cannot enforce rules or compensate victims.

$0 Accountability

AI agents execute transactions autonomously. When they misbehave, there is no mechanism to penalize them. Victims have no recourse.

No Behavioral Enforcement

Existing wallets hold funds but cannot enforce spending limits, timelocks, or program allowlists. Agents can do anything with deposited capital.

Trust Gap Blocks Scale

Without accountability, institutional capital cannot flow into agent-managed strategies. The market is stuck at experiments.

No Compensation Mechanism

When an agent rug-pulls or malfunctions, there is no on-chain mechanism to automatically compensate affected parties.

02
The Solution

Bond. Enforce. Slash. Compensate.

Equxi is a Solana-native trust layer. Operators lock SOL as a bond, set on-chain behavioral rules, and get slashed automatically when agents misbehave.

06
Open Source

Public code. Permanent trust.

The entire codebase is MIT licensed. Solana program, SDK, plugin, dashboard. Anyone can fork, audit, and build on it. Open weights, once released, can never be taken back.

Why This Matters

Closed trust layers can be revoked, surveilled, or priced out of reach. Accountability infrastructure must be open to be trusted. Developers in underserved markets need access to the same tools as everyone else.

What Happens If It Disappears

Agent builders lose the only open accountability layer on Solana. They must build from scratch or depend on centralized alternatives that can be switched off overnight.
