How It Works

Five steps to on-chain accountability

From registration to compensation, every action is enforced by the Solana program. No oracles needed. No manual intervention.

Register

Create an on-chain agent identity linked to your operator wallet.

Bond

Lock SOL as a security deposit. This is your accountability guarantee.

Enforce

Set spending limits, timelocks, and program allowlists on-chain.

Slash

When rules break, the bond is penalized automatically.

Compensate

Victims receive payment from the slashed bond. No courts needed.
