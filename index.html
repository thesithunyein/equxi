How It Works

Five steps from identity to compensation

One side raises the cost of lying. The other gets to price that cost.

Register

An on-chain identity bound to the wallet that owns it.

Bond

SOL locked as a deposit. Only the operator can withdraw it.

Enforce

Spend limits, timelocks, allowlists — on-chain, not in a config file.

Slash

A broken rule moves collateral into a program-owned vault, not to an admin.

Compensate

The injured party is paid out of escrow. No court, no claim form.
